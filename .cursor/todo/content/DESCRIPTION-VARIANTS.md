- ✅ Use specific tech stack (TypeScript, React, Node)
- ✅ Emphasize "Engineer" title

## 📝 Messaging Strategy Notes

### What to ALWAYS Include:
1. ✅ Link to DevStage.io with "Backstage" + "IDP" keywords
2. ✅ Link to Żentała.agency with "MVP" keyword
3. ✅ "0-to-1" or "early-stage" concept
4. ✅ "Tech generalist" positioning
5. ✅ Skills list: full-stack, DevOps, UX, IoT, product management

### What to AVOID:
1. ❌ "Starting things" (suggests not finishing)
2. ❌ Promising specific timelines ("weeks")
3. ❌ Mentioning AI explicitly (quality concerns)
4. ❌ "Help engineering teams" (limits audience)
5. ❌ Too detailed "messy phase" if targeting externals
6. ❌ Separating Żentała.agency as "different" work (it's the same 0-to-1 value)

### Positioning Framework:
```
SPECIALTY → SKILLS → AVAILABILITY
├─ Backstage/IDP at DevStage.io
├─ Tech generalist (list skills)
└─ MVP projects at Żentała.agency
```
